Holy Trinity Parish

Roman Catholic Parish of the Holy Trinity (Parafia pw. Trójcy Świętej) · ul. Krzycka 42-44, 53-020 Wrocław, Poland

Welcome to our parish!

All Holy Masses in our church are celebrated in Polish. Visitors, students and international residents of Wrocław are always warmly welcome to join us in prayer and take part in the liturgy.

Looking for Mass in English? Visit the Pastoral Centre for English-speakers – www.pastoralcentre.pl (opens in a new tab) or see the details below.

Above the main altar you will see the golden inscription Gloria tibi Trinitas – “Glory to You, O Holy Trinity”.

A place to pray – open all day

Our Lady of Fatima Chapel

Whether you live in Wrocław or are just passing through, you are warmly invited to stop by and pray. Our Chapel of Our Lady of Fatima is open every day from the first to the last Mass – a quiet place for personal prayer, no matter what language you pray in.

Sundays: from 7:00 until the 19:30 Mass (July – August: until 18:00) · Weekdays: from 6:30 until the 18:30 Mass

Address: ul. Krzycka 42-44, Wrocław – get directions (opens in a new tab)

Looking for Mass in English?

Holy Mass in English in Wrocław

The Sunday Mass in English is celebrated by the Franciscan Friars at the Pastoral Centre for English-speakers (opens in a new tab), in the Church of St Charles Borromeo (kościół św. Karola Boromeusza).

  • Sundays & Holy Days of Obligation16:00
  • July & August18:00
  • Addressul. Krucza 58, 53-411 Wrocław
  • Confession in EnglishBefore the Sunday Mass, or at another time by arrangement with the priest.

www.pastoralcentre.pl (opens in a new tab) Directions to ul. Krucza 58 (opens in a new tab)

Please check the Pastoral Centre’s website before your visit; it also announces English services at Christmas, during Lent and in the Easter Triduum.
